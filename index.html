09Logo Story
Part 04 · Logo System
¶ 09 — The Idea Behind the Mark

The logo of movement.

The ILS logo was born from one simple idea: to give shape to the act of moving the world. The globe represents reach. The orbital lines represent direction. The letters anchor the mark in a confident corporate identity.

It is intentionally quiet — engineered to read clearly at any size, on any surface, in any language. Like the company it represents, it does its job without raising its voice.

"Moving the world, one shipment at a time."

29Social Media
Part 07 · Brand In Use · Application 08 / 08
¶ 29 — Application 08

Social media.

A disciplined system of three story-format templates — every post follows the same anatomy. Click any template on the right to preview it on-device.

¶ The Right Procedure

Three templates. One anatomy.

Every approved ILS social post — without exception — follows the same five-step anatomy. This is what keeps the feed feeling like one company, not five different brands.

The 5-step anatomy

1
ILS lockup — top, centred or top-left The horizontal lockup sits at the head of every post. Never the element mark alone; never re-coloured. Minimum 48 px on a 1080-wide canvas.
2
One editorial headline — display weight A single, confident line in display sans (Inter Bold or equivalent). The second line shifts to --blue for visual rhythm. Never more than 6 words.
3
Supporting line + body copy Two sentences maximum. Lead with the value, end with a number. Set in Inter Regular at 28 px on the 1080 canvas.
4
Service pills or hero photography Either icon pills (Air · Sea · Land · Warehousing · Customs) or a single high-quality, brand-tinted image. Never both.
5
CTA + location footer "Request a Quote · ilsmtc.com" left, location chip ("Dubai, UAE") right. Always blue panel. Always paper-aeroplane icon.
— Brand discipline
  • Use only 1080 × 1920 px story-format (9:16)
  • Lead with ILS Blue or pure white — never a third hue
  • Image overlay tint = ILS Blue at 45% multiply
  • Do not invent new headline fonts or colours
  • Do not crop the lockup or remove the CTA panel
